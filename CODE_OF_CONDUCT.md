# Code of conduct

SpeakNote contributors, maintainers, and community members must help keep the
project welcoming, safe, and useful.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Respect privacy. Never post another person's recordings, recognized content,
  credentials, or identifying details.
- Make space for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing,
deliberate disruption, and retaliation are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

For ordinary project concerns, contact the maintainer through a
[GitHub Discussion](https://github.com/nnnc8/SpeakNote/discussions). Do not post
sensitive personal details. Security issues must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer may edit or remove content, close or lock threads, reject
contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed
to protect the community. Reports will be considered as privately and fairly as
the available GitHub channel allows.
